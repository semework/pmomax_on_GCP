# Supporting File 2: Product evidence and delivery readiness

Prepared for the HFS Services-as-Software Awards 2026. Updated May 19, 2026.

## Evidence from the current product

The live PMOMax product demonstrates:

- a complete structured initiation workflow
- live AI assistance in project context
- linked Gantt planning
- governance, compliance, risk, budget, stakeholder, and communications sections
- stakeholder-ready exports
- built-in user guidance

## Evidence from current deployment

- PMOMax is available as a public Google Cloud Marketplace application
- Marketplace deployment packaging was completed and validated
- usage-based billing validation was completed for Marketplace go-live
- the submission package includes a one-minute executive video built from the live PMOMax experience
- product screenshots and narrated demo videos exist from the live running application

## Why this is relevant to HFS SaS criteria

- **AI-driven delivery:** AI assists document generation and review inside the live workspace
- **Agentic adaptability:** the product adapts to uploaded or pasted source material and supports iterative refinement
- **Outcome orientation:** the output is a usable initiation package, not a disconnected AI answer
- **Scalable architecture:** the initiation model is reusable across projects
- **Proven enterprise success:** the strongest current proof is product maturity, Marketplace availability, deployment validation, and operating-model evidence. Customer metrics should be added later if available, but no unsupported adoption or ROI claims are needed for this submission.
