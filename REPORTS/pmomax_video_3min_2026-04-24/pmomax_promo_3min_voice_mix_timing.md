## Mix Notes

- Keep one narrator active at a time, except for the short transition crossfades.
- Normalize final dialog bus around `-18 LUFS` integrated with true peak at or below `-2 dBTP`.
- If one voice feels too prominent, trim clip gain by `1-2 dB` rather than compressing harder.
- Keep all cuts on the `6.000` second grid so captions and video timing remain aligned.
