# PMOMax 3-Minute Video Storyboard

Source app reviewed live on 2026-04-24:
`https://pmo-architect-839982691485.us-east1.run.app/`

## Current capabilities confirmed

- Paste, drag/drop, or upload project material for parsing.
- Supported file-driven intake in the product/docs: PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, CSV, XLS/XLSX.
- `Load Demo` for a fully populated example.
- `Create` mode for starting from templates/examples.
- Structured PID workspace with canonical sections:
  - Project Info
  - Overview & Rationale
  - Objectives & KPIs
  - Scope & Deliverables
  - Assumptions, Constraints, Dependencies
  - Schedule & Gantt
  - People, Resources & Budget
  - Risks, Issues & Communications
  - Governance, Compliance & Open Questions
- AI assistant panel for contextual prompting and project-specific responses.
- Risk and Compliance shortcuts tied to the live PID context.
- Gantt visualization with schedule-oriented controls and export options.
- Export panel for Word, PDF, JSON, and ZIP outputs.
- General Notes panel for working context and audit trail.
- Built-in Help modal and User Guide modal.
