# PMOMax Corrected Presentation Deck Outline

Use this as the source text for refreshed PowerPoint decks.

## Slide 1: PMOMax

AI-assisted project initiation and governance on Google Cloud.

## Slide 2: The Problem

Projects often fail because initiation is unclear. Scope, ownership, assumptions, risks, approvals, and success criteria are frequently scattered across documents, meetings, emails, and spreadsheets.

## Slide 3: Why Initiation Matters

The earlier a team clarifies project intent, controls, and evidence, the cheaper it is to fix gaps. PMOMax focuses on this high-leverage moment before execution begins.

## Slide 4: What PMOMax Does

PMOMax helps teams turn unstructured inputs into a structured Project Initiation Document, then supports governance review, risk analysis, compliance review, and AI audit traceability.

## Slide 5: Canonical PID Model

PMOMax structures objectives, success metrics, scope, assumptions, dependencies, deliverables, milestones, risks, mitigations, approvals, stakeholders, communications, decisions, and open questions.

## Slide 6: AI-Assisted Creation

Create Agent and AI-assisted workflows help draft, refine, and improve PID content while keeping humans responsible for review and approval.

## Slide 7: Governance Review

PMOMax surfaces missing owners, incomplete approvals, vague success criteria, weak decision trails, incomplete risk details, and unclear scope boundaries.

## Slide 8: Risk and Compliance Support

PMOMax helps identify risk and compliance gaps so teams can review issues earlier and gather better evidence for governance stakeholders.

## Slide 9: AI Audit Traceability

Supported AI-assisted outputs include `_auditMeta` and `_auditTrace`, with structured logs emitted to the customer-controlled runtime logging environment.

## Slide 10: Customer-Controlled Deployment

PMOMax is designed for Google Cloud Marketplace/customer-tenant deployment. Data, logs, and audit evidence remain under customer control by default.

## Slide 11: Optional Audit Backends

Customers can optionally configure GCS, BigQuery, or Firestore for additional audit retrieval patterns. These backends are customer-managed and not required for zero-configuration operation.

## Slide 12: What PMOMax Does Not Claim

PMOMax should not be described as currently implementing A2A runtime integration, native Google Docs/Sheets add-ins, immutable audit logging by default, central customer audit storage, or a fully autonomous hard enforcement engine.

## Slide 13: Current Release Direction

The current release improves Create Agent, governance workflows, compliance support, and AI audit logging while preserving a lightweight customer-controlled deployment model.

## Slide 14: Outcome

PMOMax helps teams make project initiation clearer, more auditable, and more execution-ready.

## Slide 15: Call to Action

Use PMOMax to move from scattered project intent to structured project governance before execution begins.
