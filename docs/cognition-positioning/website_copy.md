## Enterprise Governance Section

AI initiatives do not fail only because models are weak. They fail because scope, ownership, decision authority, review obligations, and audit evidence are unclear. Enterprise Cognitive Governance gives teams a structured way to define what AI may touch, what it may decide, and who owns the outcome.

## Cognition Section

Modern AI systems perform cognitive work: interpreting context, drafting plans, reviewing risks, summarizing evidence, and recommending actions. That work needs boundaries. PMOMax helps create those boundaries through structured project initiation records: scope, objectives, stakeholders, RACI, risks, mitigations, constraints, approvals, compliance notes, and trace metadata.

## PMOMax Positioning

PMOMax is an AI-assisted Project Initiation Document and governance workspace. It helps teams convert scattered project context into structured governance artifacts that can be reviewed by PMO, risk, compliance, procurement, security, and executive stakeholders.
