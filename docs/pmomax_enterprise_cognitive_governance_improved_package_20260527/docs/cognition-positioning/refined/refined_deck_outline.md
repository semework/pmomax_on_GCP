# Refined 10-Slide Deck Outline

## Slide 1 — The Shift From AI Tools to AI Workflows

**Message:** Enterprise AI is moving from isolated prompts to workflow-integrated systems that can plan, recommend, and coordinate.

**Visual:** Enterprise AI evolution timeline.

**Speaker note:** The risk is no longer just hallucination. The risk is delegated action without clear boundaries.

## Slide 2 — The Governance Gap

**Message:** Current governance often starts too late: after the workflow is already designed or deployed.

**Visual:** Downstream monitoring vs upstream governance split.

## Slide 3 — The Three Questions

**Message:** Every AI-enabled workflow needs three answers: what AI touches, what it decides, and who owns the outcome.

**Visual:** Three connected cards around a governance core.

## Slide 4 — Enterprise Cognitive Governance

**Message:** Katalyst Street defines Enterprise Cognitive Governance as the structured layer for boundaries, ownership, escalation, and auditability.

**Visual:** Governance stack diagram.

## Slide 5 — PMOMax as the Upstream Control Artifact

**Message:** PMOMax captures the initiation record that defines scope, risk, stakeholders, approvals, and decision context.

**Visual:** PMOMax before agent execution.

## Slide 6 — Cognitive Boundaries and Authority

**Message:** Enterprises must define what agents can access, recommend, decide, and escalate.

**Visual:** Boundary schema flow.

## Slide 7 — Cognitive Traceability

**Message:** AI-enabled workflows need structured trace context: request, source, model/tool call, warnings, review state, and exportable artifact.

**Visual:** Trace protocol diagram.

## Slide 8 — Regulated Industry Use Cases

**Message:** The highest-value use cases are healthcare, finance, government, and enterprise transformation offices.

**Visual:** Four vertical cards.

## Slide 9 — Product Roadmap

**Message:** PMOMax evolves from PID governance into AI workflow governance: audit metadata, escalation, policies, templates, and integrations.

**Visual:** Roadmap lanes.

## Slide 10 — Commercial Offering

**Message:** PMOMax offers a cloud-deployable governance workspace plus regulated-industry templates and advisory support.

**Visual:** SaaS + Marketplace + advisory model.
