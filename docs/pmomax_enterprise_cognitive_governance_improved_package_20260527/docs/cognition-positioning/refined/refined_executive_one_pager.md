# PMOMax and Enterprise Cognitive Governance

## The problem

Enterprise AI is shifting from isolated copilots toward agentic systems that can plan, summarize, prioritize, recommend, trigger workflows, and coordinate across tools. This creates a governance problem that begins before execution: organizations must define what AI can touch, what it can decide, and who owns the outcome.

## The upstream governance gap

Most AI governance approaches focus downstream on monitoring, model evaluation, security, or policy documentation. Those are necessary, but they often begin after the workflow is already designed. PMOMax focuses on the upstream control point: project initiation and workflow definition.

## The Katalyst Street contribution

Katalyst Street can define and commercialize Enterprise Cognitive Governance: the structured architecture, documentation, review, and audit layer around enterprise AI and agentic workflows.

## PMOMax role

PMOMax serves as a governance control artifact for AI-enabled initiatives. It structures scope, objectives, stakeholders, constraints, risks, dependencies, decisions, approvals, and audit context into an exportable record.

## The Three Questions

1. What does AI touch?
2. What does AI decide?
3. Who owns the outcome?

These questions convert abstract AI governance into practical enterprise controls.
