# Google / IBM Readiness Memo

## Google positioning

For Google, emphasize cloud-native AI governance, Marketplace deployability, enterprise AI readiness, and compatibility with agentic ecosystems.

### Recommended Google-facing message

PMOMax provides governance infrastructure for AI-enabled enterprise initiatives, helping teams define scope, ownership, risk, review checkpoints, and exportable audit context before agentic workflows are executed.

### Google strengths to emphasize

- Marketplace packaging.
- Cloud-native deployment posture.
- AI-assisted governance artifact creation.
- Enterprise audit metadata concepts.
- Future compatibility with agentic workflow ecosystems.

### Avoid for Google

- claims of current A2A interoperability unless implemented,
- claims of complete autonomous governance enforcement,
- claims of ISO certification or compliance guarantees.

## IBM positioning

For IBM, emphasize governance, regulated workflows, operational controls, auditability, and enterprise accountability.

### Recommended IBM-facing message

PMOMax helps enterprise teams convert AI initiative intent into structured governance records: boundaries, decision ownership, risk, approval context, and audit-ready documentation.

### IBM strengths to emphasize

- Governance-first architecture.
- Enterprise workflow accountability.
- Regulated industry relevance.
- Human review and escalation model.
- Potential integration with IBM Cloud and watsonx governance ecosystems.

### Avoid for IBM

- futuristic cognition rhetoric without demos,
- unsupported compliance claims,
- implying PMOMax replaces IBM governance tooling.

## Meeting recommendation

Lead with PMOMax as an upstream governance and documentation layer, not as a competing AI model or GRC replacement. Show how it can complement cloud AI platforms by making AI initiatives easier to scope, review, approve, and audit.
