# Investor Positioning Memo

## Core thesis

AI is moving from chat interfaces into operational systems. As AI systems become more agentic, enterprises will need infrastructure that defines boundaries, ownership, escalation, and auditability before workflows are executed.

Katalyst Street is positioned to own the upstream governance layer for enterprise AI and agentic workflows.

## Why now

The market has many model providers, orchestration frameworks, observability tools, and GRC platforms. The missing layer is a structured initiation and governance artifact that translates business intent into governed AI workflow boundaries.

## Why PMOMax

PMOMax already operates at the point where governance should begin: project initiation. It captures objectives, scope, risks, assumptions, constraints, stakeholders, approvals, decisions, and exportable governance artifacts. This makes it a credible control layer for AI-enabled transformation programs.

## Differentiation

PMOMax is not trying to replace foundation models, orchestration frameworks, or GRC systems. It complements them by helping enterprises define what AI is allowed to do, who owns decisions, and what must be reviewed before execution.

## Defensibility

The defensibility is not in generic AI generation. It is in:

- governance schemas,
- cognitive boundary models,
- audit metadata structures,
- enterprise workflow templates,
- regulated-industry blueprints,
- Marketplace distribution,
- domain-specific implementation patterns.

## Revenue paths

1. SaaS subscriptions for PMOMax governance workspaces.
2. Enterprise deployments through cloud marketplaces.
3. Governance blueprint packages for regulated industries.
4. Advisory and implementation services.
5. Premium auditability and compliance-readiness features.

## Investor-safe one-liner

Katalyst Street builds governance infrastructure for enterprise AI and agentic workflows, starting with PMOMax: an AI-assisted workspace for structuring, reviewing, and auditing project initiation and AI workflow accountability.
