## Three Questions section

Before AI acts, the enterprise needs clear answers:

1. What does AI touch?
2. What does AI decide?
3. Who owns the outcome?

PMOMax turns these questions into structured project initiation records, review checkpoints, and exportable governance artifacts.

## Product section

PMOMax is an AI-assisted governance workspace for project initiation and agentic workflow oversight. It helps teams define scope, constraints, risks, stakeholders, approvals, dependencies, and decision ownership in one structured record.
