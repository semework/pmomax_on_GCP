# PMOMax Cognition Mapping

## Positioning Principle

PMOMax should be framed as a structured cognitive control artifact for project initiation and governance. It does not currently provide a complete autonomous governance engine. Its credible strength is that it structures the human, project, risk, approval, and traceability context that enterprise AI workflows need before execution.

## Website-Safe Summary

PMOMax helps enterprises structure AI-assisted project initiation into reviewable governance records: scope, objectives, KPIs, stakeholders, risks, mitigations, approvals, decisions, compliance notes, and audit trace metadata. It provides an upstream control artifact for teams that need accountability before AI workflows scale.

## Deck-Safe Summary

PMOMax is not just a PID generator. It is a project-level cognitive control artifact: a structured record of what the AI-enabled initiative is intended to do, what it may not do, who owns it, what risks exist, what approvals are required, and what trace evidence is available for review.
