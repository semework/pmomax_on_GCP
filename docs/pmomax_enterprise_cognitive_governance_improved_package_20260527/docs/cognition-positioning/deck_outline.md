# Deck Outline: Enterprise Cognitive Governance

## Slide 1: Rise of Machine Cognition

- **Key message:** AI is moving from content generation to cognitive work.
- **Visual recommendation:** Layered transition from assistants to workflows to agentic systems.
- **Speaker notes:** Emphasize interpretation, planning, review, and decision support.
- **Safe claims:** AI systems increasingly support cognitive workloads; governance must adapt.

## Slide 2: Governance Crisis

- **Key message:** Enterprises cannot scale AI without accountability.
- **Visual recommendation:** Risk map showing scope drift, goal drift, unclear ownership, weak audit.
- **Speaker notes:** Governance gaps emerge before production.
- **Safe claims:** PMOMax helps document project-level governance context.

## Slide 3: Upstream Governance Gap

- **Key message:** Governance must start before execution.
- **Visual recommendation:** Funnel from idea to PID to review to execution.
- **Speaker notes:** Model inventories and monitoring are insufficient without initiation records.
- **Safe claims:** PMOMax is a structured initiation workspace.

## Slide 4: The Three Questions

- **Key message:** What does AI touch? What does AI decide? Who owns the outcome?
- **Visual recommendation:** Three-card model with central governance core.
- **Speaker notes:** These questions establish cognitive boundaries and accountability.
- **Safe claims:** PMOMax captures scope, authority, and ownership context.

## Slide 5: Enterprise Cognitive Governance Architecture

- **Key message:** Governance is a layer between AI capability and enterprise accountability.
- **Visual recommendation:** Enterprise cognitive governance stack diagram.
- **Speaker notes:** Show foundation models, orchestration, governance, PID, human accountability, audit.
- **Safe claims:** PMOMax maps to the PID/control-artifact layer.
