# PMOMax PID as ISO/IEC 42001 Supporting Documented Information

**Document version:** v1.0  
**Document date:** May 19, 2026  
**Prepared for:** Enterprise AI governance, PMO, compliance, risk, procurement, and digital transformation review

## Executive Summary

ISO/IEC 42001 is an international management system standard for Artificial Intelligence Management Systems. Public ISO descriptions explain that the standard supports responsible AI governance, risk and opportunity management, transparency, accountability, traceability, and lifecycle oversight for organizations that provide or use AI-based products or services.

> **Readiness positioning:** PMOMax supports structured project initiation evidence for AI governance discussions. It is not a certification tool and does not guarantee ISO/IEC 42001 compliance.

PMOMax is not an ISO/IEC 42001 certification tool and does not make an organization compliant. PMOMax can, however, help organizations structure project initiation information that may support AI governance readiness and AIMS documented information.

## Where PMOMax Helps

The PMOMax PID is especially relevant where enterprise teams need a consistent project-level record of:

- AI project scope, objectives, intended outcomes, assumptions, constraints, and dependencies.
- Named sponsors, owners, stakeholders, and role accountability.
- Project risks, mitigations, contingencies, compliance notes, and privacy/security considerations.
- Human oversight signals such as governance gates, signoff requirements, decision records, and review cadence.
- Lifecycle planning through milestones, work breakdown tasks, dependencies, resources, KPIs, and follow-up actions.
- Traceability metadata for AI-assisted outputs where audit settings are enabled.

The practical value for enterprise buyers is straightforward: PMOMax helps convert early AI project planning into structured, reviewable, exportable documentation that can be used by PMO, risk, compliance, procurement, digital transformation, and AI governance teams.

## Best Use

The best use of PMOMax in ISO/IEC 42001 readiness is as a supporting documentation workflow inside a broader governance program. Enterprises should use PMOMax PID outputs as project-level evidence for internal review, readiness workshops, control mapping, and conversations with qualified ISO/IEC 42001 consultants, auditors, or certification bodies.

PMOMax does not replace the organization's AI Management System, legal review, risk management program, impact assessment process, internal audit program, management review, or certification process. Full ISO/IEC 42001 readiness requires organization-specific policies, procedures, controls, records, oversight, monitoring, and independent professional review.
